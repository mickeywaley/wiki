解决办法

依次执行以下命令：

 mv /var/lib/dpkg/info/ /var/lib/dpkg/info_old/
 
 mkdir /var/lib/dpkg/info/
 
 apt-get update
 
 apt-get -f install
 
 mv /var/lib/dpkg/info/* /var/lib/dpkg/info_old/
 
 rm -rf /var/lib/dpkg/info
 
 mv /var/lib/dpkg/info_old/ /var/lib/dpkg/info/
